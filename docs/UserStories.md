# User Stories

## 1. Strategic Consultant

1. As a user, I want the AI to ask about my professional background so it can adjust the complexity of the business terminology it uses.
2. As a founder, I want the AI to ask for my starting budget so it can filter out startup paths that are financially unrealistic.
3. As an entrepreneur, I want to specify my desired launch date so the AI can prioritize minimum viable product steps.
4. As a solo founder, I want the AI to assess my skill gaps so it can highlight which parts of the startup path I may need to outsource.

## 2. Failure Vault

5. As a user in financial services, I want to see a list of failed startups in my niche and the root causes of their collapse.
6. As a founder in food and drink, I want the AI to tell me whether a sub-category is oversaturated with competitors.
7. As a user, I want the AI to flag common industry traps during the initial conversation.
8. As a business owner, I want the AI to show me successful pivots in the technology sector so I can understand alternative paths if my first idea fails.

## 3. Market Entry and Trends

9. As a marketer in beauty and personal care, I want a report on the top consumer behavior shifts in the current market.
10. As a strategist, I want to know how media and content trends are affecting the retail sector.
11. As a founder in travel and tourism, I want to understand how AI and VR are changing the booking experience.
12. As a user in packaging, I want the AI to summarize sustainable material trends and their costs.

## 4. Startup Path

13. As a user, I want my startup path broken into the phases of research, legal, build, launch, and scale.
14. As a founder in TV, phone, and internet, I want a localized checklist of permits and licenses required to start.
15. As a non-technical founder in sports, gaming, and entertainment, I want the AI to recommend a tech stack based on expected scalability needs.
16. As a user, I want each step in my path to include success criteria so I know when I am ready to move forward.

## 5. Marketing Growth Engine

17. As a marketer, I want the AI to rank channels such as SEO, TikTok, PR, and email for my category.
18. As a user, I want the AI to generate detailed buyer personas based on current market data.
19. As a user, I want a three-month content calendar aligned with industry trends.
20. As a founder, I want the AI to generate high-converting ad copy for Meta and Google Ads.

## 6. Competitor and Growth Intelligence

21. As a user, I want the AI to perform a real-time SWOT analysis of my top competitors.
22. As a founder in ingredients, flavours, and fragrances, I want the AI to suggest a pricing strategy based on industry benchmarks.
23. As a strategist, I want the AI to identify underserved customer segments in the retail space.

## 7. Reporting and Documentation

24. As an entrepreneur, I want to export the full consultation into a structured PDF for investors.
25. As a project manager, I want to view my startup path as a timeline so I can manage my launch schedule.
26. As a founder, I want the AI to generate a 10-slide pitch deck outline based on the market research.
27. As a user, I want a one-page executive summary that I can share with potential partners.

## 8. Agentic Monitoring

28. As a user in government or insurance, I want to be notified when a new law affects my startup path.
29. As a user, I want the AI to alert me if a trend I depend on starts to decline.
30. As a user, I want the AI to proactively follow up when new failure cases are found that affect my roadmap.

---

## Why This Set Is Broad

- **Breadth** — It covers discovery, market intelligence, execution, marketing, and monitoring.
- **Depth** — It includes both strategic guidance and concrete outputs such as ad copy and roadmaps.
- **Interactivity** — The AI adapts to user constraints and continuously updates guidance over time.
