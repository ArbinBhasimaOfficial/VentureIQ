# Research planning checklist

Use this checklist to turn an open market question into a focused research brief.

- Define the decision this research should support.
- Name the audience, category, geography, and time horizon.
- Separate what is already known from what is uncertain.
- List the signals that would change the decision.
- Identify the best primary and secondary evidence sources.
- Decide how findings will be shared with stakeholders.
- Set a deadline for the first useful recommendation.
- End with a clear owner and next action.
